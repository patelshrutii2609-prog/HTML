Data Science

from wikipedia the free encyclopedia

Not to be confused with information science or computer science.

Data science is an interdisciplinary academic field [1]that uses statistics,scientific computing,scientific methods,processing,scientificvisualization,algorithms and systems to extrapolate knowledge and insights from potentially noisy,structured or unstructured data.[2]

Data science also integrates domain knowledge from the underlying application domain(e.g.,natural science ,information technology,and medicine).[3]Data science is multifaceted and can be describe as a science,a research paradigm,a research method ,a discipline,a workflow and a profession.[4]

Data science is "a concept to unify statistics,data analysis ,informatics,and their related methods"to "understand and analyze actual phenomena"with data.[5]It uses techniques and theories drawn from many fields within the context of mathematics,statistics,computer science,information science,and domain knowledge.[6]However,data science is different from computer science and information science.Turing awards winnwr Jim Gray imagined dara science as a "foutrh paradigm" of science(empirical,theoretical,computational and now data-driven)and asserted that "everyone about science is changing because of the impact of information technology" and the data deluge.[7][8] A data scientist is a professional who creates programminh code and combines it with statistical knowledge to create insights from data.[9]
